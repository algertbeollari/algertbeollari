Scout the following for risks and blind spots. Structure:
1. Risks that are stated or obvious
2. Risks that are NOT mentioned but likely apply (the blind spots) — this is the important part
3. What single piece of missing information would most change the risk picture
4. One mitigation per material risk

Be specific — name the exact failure mode, not a generic category.
