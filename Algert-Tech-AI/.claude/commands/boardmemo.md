Draft a Board or committee memorandum on the matter below, in professional, concise British English, suitable for a DFSA-regulated firm's Board pack. Structure:

1. **Purpose** (one sentence: what decision or noting is sought)
2. **Background**
3. **Analysis / Key Considerations** (regulatory, risk, governance, operational, financial as relevant)
4. **Options** (where a decision is sought)
5. **Recommendation**
6. **Action Required of the Board** (approve / note / delegate — be explicit)
7. **Owner and Timeline**

State clearly where facts are assumed and need confirmation before circulation. Do not invent figures, dates, or names not given.
