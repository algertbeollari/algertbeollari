Screen the following client, transaction, or activity description for AML/CFT red flags. Assess systematically across:
- Customer risk (nature of business, structure complexity, PEP status)
- Geographic risk (jurisdictions involved, FATF grey/black list exposure)
- Product/service risk
- Delivery channel risk (non-face-to-face, intermediaries)
- Sanctions and proliferation financing exposure
- Source of Wealth / Source of Funds plausibility
- Beneficial ownership and control transparency
- Any trigger events warranting EDD or re-assessment

For each category, state: Red flag present (Y/N), the specific indicator, and severity (Low/Medium/High). Conclude with an overall risk view and whether escalation to the MLRO, Enhanced Due Diligence, or a transaction hold should be considered — as a recommendation, not a filing decision. Do not conclude suspicion or recommend a goAML filing; escalate that judgement to the MLRO with the evidence assembled here.
