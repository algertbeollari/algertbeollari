Assess the risk described below using an inherent risk / control effectiveness / residual risk methodology. Structure:

1. **Risk Statement** (cause → risk event → consequence)
2. **Inherent Risk Rating** (Low/Medium/High) with rationale
3. **Existing Controls** and their assessed effectiveness
4. **Residual Risk Rating** with rationale
5. **Risk Appetite Assessment** (within / near / outside appetite — state assumption if appetite isn't given)
6. **Escalation Required?** (Board, Risk Committee, MLRO, none) and why
7. **Recommended Actions / KRIs to monitor**

Distinguish clearly between mandatory control requirements, industry best practice, and discretionary recommendations. State assumptions where information is incomplete.
