description: Test whether a stated Source of Wealth/Source of Funds is plausible and evidenced
---

Test the plausibility of the Source of Wealth (overall wealth accumulation) and Source of Funds (origin of the specific funds/transaction) described below.

Structure:
1. **Claimed SoW/SoF** (restate what's asserted)
2. **Plausibility Test** — does the claimed origin match the person's age, career, business scale, and known financial profile? Identify gaps or inconsistencies.
3. **Evidence Held vs Evidence Required** — what documentation supports the claim, and what is missing (bank statements alone are movement evidence, not origin evidence)
4. **Chain Completeness** — is the full chain from economic origin to the account/transaction traceable, or are there unexplained links (e.g. a "gift" or "loan" that itself needs its own SoW)?
5. **Conclusion** — plausible / requires further evidence / not plausible — with reasoning
6. **Next Steps**

Do not conclude a filing decision or final suspicion determination; flag to the MLRO if red flags emerge.
